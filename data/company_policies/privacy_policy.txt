
NOVATECH SOLUTIONS PVT. LTD.
INTERNAL DATA PRIVACY POLICY
Version: 1.0
Status: Fictional Sample — For Testing Only

1. PERSONAL DATA COLLECTION
NovaTech collects customer names, email addresses, phone numbers,
and account information to provide its digital services.

2. CONSENT MANAGEMENT
NovaTech obtains customer consent before processing personal data.
Customers can withdraw their consent by contacting customer support.

3. DATA BREACH RESPONSE
NovaTech investigates suspected personal data breaches and informs
affected customers when necessary.

4. DATA RETENTION
NovaTech retains customer personal data for business and operational
purposes. Data is deleted when no longer required.

5. CUSTOMER RIGHTS
Customers may request access to their personal data, correction of
inaccurate information, or deletion of their personal data.

6. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
Customers can submit privacy complaints through the customer
support team.

7. CHILDREN'S PERSONAL DATA
NovaTech requires parental consent before processing personal data
belonging to children.

8. SECURITY SAFEGUARDS
NovaTech uses access controls, encryption, and employee security
training to protect personal data.
